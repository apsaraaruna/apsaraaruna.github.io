Fill in, check, print

Pick a date and payee and type the amount. The preview updates as you type, on top of an image of the cheque leaf.

  • Cheque number counts up after every print
  • Long amounts wrap neatly onto the second line
  • Add a memo, like an invoice number, for your records

Line it up perfectly with your printer

Every printer feeds paper a little differently. On the Fields page you can drag each field into place, nudge it with arrow keys, and set its width, font size, alignment and bold.

  • Positions in real millimetres
  • Hide any field you don't want printed
  • Reset a single field or all of them

What you see is what prints

Before anything goes to the printer you see the full sheet, with the cheque position shown. The cheque background is only a guide on screen. Just the text prints onto your cheque.

  • Choose the printer and copies
  • Use the Windows print dialog or print directly
  • Test print with a cheque outline on plain paper

Questions

Can't find your answer? Get in touch.

Does it only work with RAKBANK cheques?

It comes set up for the standard RAKBANK cheque layout. You can move every field on the Fields page, so it can be adjusted to other UAE bank cheques too. For a ready-made layout for your bank, contact me.

Where is my data stored?

On your own computer, in Documents\Cheque Writer\cheque-data.json, with an automatic backup copy. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Uninstalling keeps your data.

Which printers work?

Any Windows printer that can take a cheque leaf, through the manual feed slot or tray. You can print on a cheque-size page or on A4 with the cheque placed at the top.

The text isn't lined up with my cheque. What do I do?

Print the alignment ruler on a sample cheque and enter where the cheque's left and top edges land. Then do a test print and fine-tune any field on the Fields page. Also check the Windows print dialog is set to 100% scale and no margins.

Is it really free?

Yes. If your business needs a custom version, such as another bank, your own layout or integration with your accounting, I can build it for you.

Can I move it to another PC?

Yes. Use Backup on the Payees tab to save everything to one file, then Restore on the new PC.
